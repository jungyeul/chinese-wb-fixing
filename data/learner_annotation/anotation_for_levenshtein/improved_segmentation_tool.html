Chinese Segmentation Comparison Tool

How to Use:

  • Upload Files: Select your target and source CSV files
  • Review Each Pair: Compare target vs source segmentation (differences are highlighted)
  • Judge Consistency: Press Y (consistent), N (inconsistent), or H (hard to decide)
  • Revise Anytime: You can change your judgment when revisiting any row
  • Make Corrections: For inconsistent pairs, input the correct segmentation (source text pre-filled)
  • Add Memos: For hard-to-decide cases, add notes for future reference
  • Save: Use Ctrl+S to save corrections or memos
  • Cancel: Press ESC to cancel editing corrections or memos
  • Navigate: Use Previous/Next buttons or Ctrl+← / Ctrl+→
  • Undo: Press "Undo Last Input" or Ctrl+Z if you make a mistake
  • Export: Save your results when finished
